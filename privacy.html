Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains how All-in-One Tool and Fast Uploader ("the app") use Google user data. The app is a desktop program that runs on the user's own Windows computer.

What data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for permission to manage your YouTube account. With that permission it:

  • reads your channel name, picture and subscriber count, to show which channel is signed in;
  • uploads the videos and thumbnails you choose, with the title, description, tags and schedule you choose;
  • deletes a video from your channel only when you ask it to.

Where the data is stored

Your sign-in (OAuth token) and your settings are stored only on your own computer, encrypted for your Windows user. The app has no server. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share your Google data with anyone. Data goes only between your computer and Google/YouTube.

Google API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to remove access

Remove a channel in the app, or remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's folder removes all stored data from your computer.
